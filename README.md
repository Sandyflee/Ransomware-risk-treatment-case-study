# Ransomware Risk Treatment Case Study

## Northbridge Community Health

### Project Overview
This project presents a Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) case study assessing ransomware risks for a fictional three-clinic healthcare provider.

The assessment examines risk identification, risk scoring, treatment prioritisation, budget allocation, financial exposure, residual risks and incident response planning.

The case study uses the 2021 Ireland Health Service Executive (HSE) ransomware incident as its real-world reference.

### Key Activities
- Identified and assessed four ransomware-related risks.
- Developed a risk register with likelihood and impact scoring.
- Selected risk treatment controls within a $100,000 budget.
- Evaluated risk avoidance, mitigation and financial transfer.
- Calculated retained financial exposure under illustrative insurance terms.
- Revised treatment decisions following a failed restoration test.
- Developed a 30-day risk treatment plan.
- Prepared a board-level risk communication memo.
